# Koi — a login card on an ink pond

A login card laid on a koi pond painted in sumi ink on cold-press watercolour paper. The koi swim around the card, come closer while you type, scatter on a wrong password, and bloom into colour when you get in: where they swim the paper gets wet, and their black ink runs into watercolour.

**Live:** https://madebysourasith-sudo.github.io/koi-login/

- Koi, lotus, ripples, wet paper and the bleeding pigment are WebGL2 shaders
- Demo only: any email and a password of 6+ characters signs you in
- One HTML file, no build step

Made by Sourasith with Claude Code.
